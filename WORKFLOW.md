# Make another comic

1. Find a current AI or tech story. Record its source URL, publication date, and the concrete fact you verified. Read the article rather than relying on a headline.
2. Identify a reversal: what the technology promises versus the unexpected human consequence. Keep the joke understandable without knowing a product name.
3. Write the two speech bubbles first. Panel one establishes confidence; panel two reveals an ironic result. Keep secondary labels short.
4. Fill in [the prompt template](prompts/template.txt). Specify the action, expressions, exact dialogue, and which character each speech-bubble tail belongs to.
5. Generate with GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst. Start with a 1024 × 1024 square and high quality, matching this collection.
6. Inspect the image at phone size: spelling, reading order, bubble attribution, character continuity, prices or arithmetic, cropping, and whether the visual joke works without explanation.
7. If necessary, change one specific issue and generate a sibling version. Keep the exact final prompt and original output.
8. Export a grayscale copy for this series, preserving the original. Add the image, prompt, source note, and generation settings to the repository.

## Visual direction

Two tall panels side by side on square white paper. Slightly irregular black ink borders, expressive round-headed people, minimal setting details, and large hand-lettered dialogue. The setup feels calm; the payoff is exaggerated. Notification cards, receipts, or screens can provide a secondary joke.

Avoid packing a paragraph into a bubble. Make the main dialogue readable before adding decorative detail. Use original characters, staging, and jokes, while retaining the reference's broad format.

## Story notes for this collection

- **AI Middle Manager:** The developer's new responsibility is supervising the very bots meant to remove work.
- **The $19 Toaster:** The bot optimizes the purchase price while overlooking its own operating cost.
- **Human Power Supply:** A reported token promotion becomes an absurd feedback loop: the human consumes coffee to keep the computer working.
- **Certified Slop:** Image provenance and truth are different. The camera records a physical print accurately, but the print depicts something invented.
